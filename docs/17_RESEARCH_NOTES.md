# 17 — Research Notes

SentinelX is not intended to clone AiSOC or another product.

The engineering distinction is an evidence-first investigation loop with strict separation between deterministic detection/risk and LLM-assisted investigation/explanation.

Limitations: synthetic/local reputation data, limited scale, simplified correlation, small local model, no production SIEM connectors, no autonomous remediation, prototype ML. The full, measured list, with tradeoffs and interview Q&A, is in `docs/23_INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md`.
